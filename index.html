Bets where the best UK bookmaker price pays more than it should. Bookmakers are sharper than a simple model, so the chance used is 30% our model + 70% the bookmakers' own consensus. How the model works

  • Attack & defence strength – goals scored and conceded per game (home/away split plus overall), pulled slightly towards the league average for small samples. Each league is modelled against its own averages. Where season xG is available it's blended in 50/50.
  • Last 5 form – recent games weighted heavier (newest ×5, oldest ×1). Hot teams get up to ~+15% on expected goals, cold teams lose the same.
  • Home advantage – built in, because home and away goal rates are modelled separately.
  • Player form – each team's top scorers and assisters. Tap a player who's out and their team's attack drops by their share of its goals (goals + half of assists), less ~40% for whoever replaces them. Mark players out yourself once team news is known.
  • Weather – heavy rain, strong wind and freezing conditions cut expected goals (roughly 2–10%).
  • Goals markets – expected goals feed a Poisson scoreline model (0–0 to 8–8) for win/draw/loss, double chance, over/under 1.5/2.5/3.5, both teams to score and the most likely score.
  • Value – edge = (30% model chance + 70% bookmaker consensus) × best sportsbook price − 1, skipping longshots over 5.0 and big model/market disagreements. The "Bookies" figure is the average UK price turned into a chance with the bookmaker's margin removed. If the model and the bookies disagree a lot, the bookies are more often right.
  • Accumulators – the chance all legs land is each leg's chance multiplied together; fair odds = 1 ÷ that chance. Legs from the same match are linked, so for those the combined figure is only a rough guide.
