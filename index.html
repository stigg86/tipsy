How accurate is Tipsy?

How the model works

  • Attack & defence strength – goals scored and conceded per game (home/away split plus overall), pulled slightly towards the league average for small samples. Each league is modelled against its own averages. For the Premier League, season xG from the FPL feed is blended in 50/50.
  • Last 5 form – recent games weighted heavier (newest ×5, oldest ×1). Hot teams get up to ~+15% on expected goals, cold teams lose the same.
  • Home advantage – built in, because home and away goal rates are modelled separately.
  • Team news – for the Premier League, absent regulars come from the FPL feed. If a top scorer is out, their team's attack drops by their share of its goals (goals + half of assists), less ~40% for whoever replaces them; each other regular out trims attack 6% and adds 3% to what they concede.
  • Weather – heavy rain, strong wind and freezing conditions cut expected goals (roughly 2–10%).
  • Club Elo – long-run team strength from clubelo.com. It counts for about half of team strength after 10 games and fades as the season builds up evidence.
  • Rest – a team playing 3 or fewer days after its last league or Champions League game loses 4% attack and concedes 3% more. Europa League and Conference League games aren't in the free data.
  • Goals markets – expected goals feed a Poisson scoreline model (0–0 to 8–8) with the Dixon–Coles correction, which adds a little to 0–0 and 1–1 and takes it from 1–0 and 0–1. That gives win/draw/loss, double chance, over/under 1.5/2.5/3.5, both teams to score and the most likely score.
  • Score predictions – the predicted score is the most likely scoreline for the most likely result. For a likely home win that's usually 1–0 or 2–1, even when 1–1 is the single most common score. Exact scores are hard: even the best-placed scoreline rarely tops 15%.
  • Bookmakers in the tips – where odds exist, we work out the expected goals the bookmakers' prices imply and blend them 50/50 with ours. Bookmakers know things our data can't see, such as rotation and late team news, so this makes the tips more accurate. The value finder still uses our model on its own, so it isn't just comparing bookmakers with themselves.
  • Value – edge = (30% model chance + 70% bookmaker consensus) × best sportsbook price − 1, skipping longshots over 5.0 and big model/market disagreements. The "Bookies" figure is the average UK price turned into a chance with the bookmaker's margin removed. If the model and the bookies disagree a lot, the bookies are more often right.
  • Accumulators – the chance all legs land is each leg's chance multiplied together; fair odds = 1 ÷ that chance. Legs from the same match are linked, so for those the combined figure is only a rough guide.

About the data

Fixtures, tables, last-5 form, top scorers, UK bookmaker odds and kick-off weather refresh automatically. Premier League team news and xG come from the official Fantasy Premier League feed, and injured, suspended or doubtful regulars are taken into account automatically. Team news for La Liga, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 isn't in the free data yet. Percentages are model estimates, not certainties. 18+ · BeGambleAware.org
