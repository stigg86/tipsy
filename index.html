How the model works

  • Attack & defence strength – half from actual goals (home/away split plus overall), half from season xG and xGA per game. xG measures chance quality, so it filters out lucky finishing and freak results. Then it's pulled slightly towards average, because 10 games is a small sample.
  • Player form – each team's players in the league's top 15 scorers or assisters, with goals, assists and goal involvements per game. Tap a player who's out and their team's attack drops by their share of its goals (goals + half of assists), less ~40% for whoever replaces them.
  • Last 5 form – points from the last five, with recent games weighted heavier (newest ×5, oldest ×1). Hot teams get up to ~+15% on expected goals, cold teams lose the same.
  • Home advantage – built in, because home and away goal rates are modelled separately.
  • Weather – heavy rain, strong wind and freezing conditions cut expected goals (roughly 2–10%), which nudges draws and unders up.
  • Other key players out – for anyone not listed (defenders, keepers): each one trims that side's attack by 6% and adds 3% to what they concede.
  • Result – expected goals feed a Poisson scoreline model (0–0 up to 8–8) to get win/draw/loss, over 2.5, both-teams-to-score and the most likely score.
